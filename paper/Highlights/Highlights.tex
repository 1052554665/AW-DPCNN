\section*{Highlights}

\begin{itemize}
	\item AW-DPCNN adaptively fuses STFT spectrograms and GADF images via contrast-guided dynamic weighting with dual-channel convolutional stimuli, suppressing destructive interference between heterogeneous representations
	\item MSCA-VGG16 integrates multi-scale convolution and squeeze-and-excitation channel attention with a compact embedding head for robust multi-granularity fault identification
	\item Achieves 99.85\% accuracy on CWRU 12~kHz drive-end bearing fault benchmark; adaptive weighting contributes 2.93\% over fixed fusion baseline
	\item Demonstrates 97.45\% cross-sensor generalization and 94.81\% cross-sampling-rate robustness with systematic ablation validation
	\item Maintains 91.2\% accuracy at 5.0~dB SNR under additive Gaussian noise, confirming real-world noise resilience
\end{itemize}
