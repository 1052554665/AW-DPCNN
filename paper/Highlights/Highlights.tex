\section*{Highlights}

\begin{itemize}
	\item AW-DPCNN fuses STFT and GADF images via contrast-guided dynamic weighting and dual-channel stimuli
	\item MSCA-VGG16 with multi-scale convolution, channel attention, and compact embedding for diagnosis
	\item Achieves 99.85\% accuracy on CWRU 12 kHz drive-end bearing fault benchmark
	\item Demonstrates cross-sensor and cross-sampling-rate generalization with systematic ablation
	\item Maintains 91.2\% accuracy at 5.0 dB SNR under additive Gaussian noise
\end{itemize}
